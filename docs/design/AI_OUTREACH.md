# AI Outreach Design Specification

## Purpose

AI Outreach transforms Scout's intelligence into personalized, context-aware customer communications.

Rather than generating generic emails, Scout should create thoughtful outreach that reflects the customer's business priorities, current initiatives, executive interests, and identified opportunities.

Every outreach draft should answer:

- Why are we reaching out?
- Why now?
- Why is this message relevant?
- What action should the recipient take?

Outreach should feel like it was written by an experienced enterprise account executive who has thoroughly researched the customer.

---

# Design Objectives

AI Outreach shall:

- Generate highly personalized communications.
- Incorporate relevant business intelligence.
- Align messaging with customer priorities.
- Recommend the appropriate communication strategy.
- Maintain a professional and consultative tone.
- Explain why each message is being recommended.
- Help sales teams start meaningful conversations.

# Outreach Overview

Display a concise summary of the outreach.

Include:

- Company
- Recipient
- Outreach Type
- Purpose
- Priority
- Recommended Timing

Users should immediately understand the objective of the communication.

---

# AI Outreach Strategy

Scout should explain:

- Why this outreach is recommended.
- Why now is an appropriate time.
- Which business signals triggered the recommendation.
- Desired business outcome.

This section should help users understand the reasoning before reviewing the draft.

---

# Audience Selection

Support selecting the intended audience.

Potential recipients include:

- Executive Sponsor
- Technical Decision Maker
- Business Leader
- Engineering Leader
- Procurement Contact
- Existing Relationship

For each recipient, Scout should explain why they are the appropriate audience.

---

# Message Objective

Clearly define the purpose of the communication.

Examples:

- Introduction
- Follow-up
- Executive engagement
- Discovery meeting
- Workshop invitation
- Opportunity discussion
- Relationship building
- Post-meeting follow-up

The objective should influence tone, structure, and call-to-action.

---

# Personalization Insights

Before generating content, summarize the customer-specific context.

Examples:

- Recent executive announcement
- AI initiative
- Hiring activity
- Product launch
- Cloud modernization
- Strategic initiative
- Public interview
- Industry trend

These insights should directly influence the generated message.

---

# Generated Draft

Scout should generate a polished outreach message.

The draft should include:

- Personalized opening
- Relevant business context
- Value-focused messaging
- Natural transition
- Clear call-to-action
- Professional closing

Messages should remain conversational, concise, and respectful.

---

# Alternative Versions

Provide multiple communication styles.

Examples:

- Executive
- Technical
- Relationship-building
- Formal
- Concise
- Warm
- Follow-up

Users should be able to choose the style that best fits the situation.

---

# Supporting Evidence

Explain why the outreach was generated.

Include:

- Relevant company developments
- Executive priorities
- Opportunity signals
- Business initiatives
- Supporting sources

Users should understand the reasoning behind the messaging.

---

# Call-to-Action Recommendations

Scout should recommend an appropriate next step.

Examples:

- Schedule discovery meeting
- Executive introduction
- Technical workshop
- Product demonstration
- Strategy discussion
- Follow-up conversation

Each recommendation should explain:

- Expected value
- Business rationale
- Recommended timing

---

# Review & Edit

Users should be able to:

- Edit any section
- Regenerate content
- Adjust tone
- Shorten or expand
- Change audience
- Save drafts
- Copy content

Scout should support human refinement rather than replacing it.

---

# Intelligence Principles

Every generated communication shall include:

- Business reasoning
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence
- Timestamp
- AI explanation

Users should understand why Scout recommends a particular message.

# Success Criteria

A successful AI Outreach page enables users to answer:

- Why are we contacting this customer?
- Why is this message relevant?
- Why now?
- What value are we offering?
- What action should the recipient take?

If users feel comfortable sending the generated draft with only minor edits, the experience has achieved its purpose.

Rather than producing generic sales emails, Scout should help users communicate with relevance, credibility, and purpose—turning intelligence into stronger customer relationships.
