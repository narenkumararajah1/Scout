# AI Outreach Design Specification

## Purpose

The AI Outreach module assists sales teams in creating personalized, relevant, and business-focused customer communications.

Rather than generating generic sales emails, Scout shall leverage company intelligence, executive insights, opportunity analysis, and meeting preparation to produce outreach that is contextual, consultative, and personalized.

The objective is to reduce writing effort while increasing outreach quality.

Every outreach generated by Scout should answer one primary question:

**"Why would this customer want to respond?"**

---

# Design Objectives

The AI Outreach experience shall:

- Personalize every communication.
- Leverage Scout intelligence.
- Improve response rates.
- Reduce manual writing.
- Maintain a professional tone.
- Encourage consultative selling.
- Keep users in control of all communications.

AI shall assist the user—not replace their judgment.

---

# Core Philosophy

Scout does not generate mass emails.

Scout generates meaningful business conversations.

Every outreach should demonstrate an understanding of:

- The customer's business
- Their current priorities
- Recent company developments
- Relevant technology initiatives
- Potential business challenges
- Appropriate Innominds capabilities

Every communication should feel researched and intentional.

# Success Criteria

A successful AI Outreach experience enables users to answer the following questions within five minutes:

- Why am I contacting this customer?
- What business value should I communicate?
- What recent developments should I reference?
- How should I personalize the outreach?
- What action should I request?
- Does this communication sound professional?
- Am I confident sending this message?

If users still feel the need to rewrite most of the generated content, the experience should be redesigned.

---

# Final Principle

AI Outreach is not about automating communication.

It is about helping sales professionals communicate more thoughtfully, more personally, and more effectively by leveraging Scout's intelligence.

Every message should strengthen relationships, demonstrate genuine understanding of the customer's business, and create opportunities for meaningful conversations.
