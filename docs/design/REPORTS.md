# Reports Design Specification

## Purpose

The Reports module transforms Scout's intelligence into polished, executive-ready deliverables.

Rather than exporting application data, Scout should generate consulting-grade reports that communicate business context, strategic insights, opportunity analysis, and recommended actions.

Every report should tell a complete business story supported by evidence, visualizations, and AI reasoning.

Every report should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What opportunities exist?
- What should we do next?

Reports should require little to no manual editing before being shared with executives or customers.

---

# Design Objectives

Reports shall:

- Transform intelligence into executive-ready narratives.
- Prioritize business understanding over raw information.
- Explain AI recommendations transparently.
- Use visualizations to improve decision-making.
- Present actionable recommendations.
- Maintain a consistent professional appearance.
- Be suitable for presentations and customer engagements.

Reports should feel like they were prepared by an experienced management consultant.

---

# Report Structure

```
Cover Page
        ↓
Executive Summary
        ↓
Key Insights
        ↓
Business Context
        ↓
Strategic Analysis
        ↓
Supporting Evidence
        ↓
Opportunity Analysis
        ↓
Recommended Actions
        ↓
Appendix (Optional)
```

Every report should progress naturally from understanding to action.

---

# Cover Page

Every report begins with a professional cover page.

Include:

- Report Title
- Company Name
- Report Type
- Generated Date
- Prepared by Scout
- Company Logo (where available)
- Confidentiality Indicator (optional)

The design should remain minimal and executive-ready.

---

# Executive Summary

Every report begins with a concise AI-generated executive briefing.

The summary should explain:

- Overall business situation
- Significant developments
- Primary opportunities
- Major risks
- Recommended next steps

The executive summary should enable leadership to understand the report within two minutes.

---

# Key Insights

Highlight the most important discoveries.

Examples:

- AI investment accelerating.
- Cloud modernization initiative identified.
- Executive leadership changes detected.
- Hiring indicates platform expansion.
- New opportunity emerging.

Each insight should include:

- Why it matters
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence

Focus on quality rather than quantity.

---

# Business Context

Provide a concise overview of the company's current business environment.

Include:

- Industry position
- Strategic priorities
- Business challenges
- Recent developments
- Technology direction
- Competitive landscape

Avoid lengthy background information that does not influence decision-making.

---

# Strategic Analysis

Explain how the collected intelligence connects into a broader business narrative.

Rather than listing isolated events, Scout should explain:

- How events relate
- Why they occurred
- What they suggest
- How the company's direction is evolving

This section should read like a consultant's strategic assessment.

---

# Supporting Evidence

Every major conclusion should be supported by evidence.

Examples:

- News articles
- Executive interviews
- Earnings calls
- Hiring activity
- Product launches
- Technology investments
- Public announcements

Each source should include:

- Description
- Date
- Relevance

Evidence should strengthen trust without overwhelming readers.

---

# Opportunity Analysis

Summarize the highest-value opportunities identified by Scout.

Each opportunity should include:

- Business Challenge
- Why It Matters
- Supporting Evidence
- Confidence
- Recommended Innominds Capability
- Estimated Business Impact

Users should understand exactly why each recommendation exists.

---

# Recommended Actions

Every report concludes with prioritized recommendations.

Examples:

Immediate

- Schedule executive outreach
- Generate Sales Playbook

Short-Term

- Prepare discovery workshop
- Monitor hiring trends

Long-Term

- Build strategic relationship
- Continue monitoring technology investments

Recommendations should explain:

- Why now
- Expected value
- Priority
- Confidence

---

# Visualizations

Reports should include meaningful visualizations where they improve understanding.

Examples:

- Opportunity Score
- Technology Landscape
- Executive Timeline
- Hiring Trends
- Strategic Initiative Timeline
- Capability Alignment
- Industry Comparison

Visualizations should complement the narrative rather than replace it.

---

# AI Transparency

Every AI-generated recommendation should identify:

- AI reasoning
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence
- Timestamp
- Source attribution

Readers should understand how Scout reached its conclusions.

---

# Writing Style

Reports should maintain:

- Professional language
- Executive-friendly tone
- Concise explanations
- Clear formatting
- Strong visual hierarchy

Reports should communicate confidence without overstating certainty.

# Success Criteria

A successful report enables readers to answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- Which opportunities exist?
- Why is Scout recommending them?
- What evidence supports these recommendations?
- What actions should be taken next?

If an executive can confidently understand the business situation and make informed decisions without opening Scout, the report has achieved its purpose.

---

# Final Principle

Scout reports should not feel like exported application screens.

They should feel like professionally prepared executive consulting deliverables that transform intelligence into understanding, understanding into strategy, and strategy into action.
