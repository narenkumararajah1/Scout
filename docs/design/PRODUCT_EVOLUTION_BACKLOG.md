# Purpose

The Product Evolution Backlog captures approved future enhancements for Scout.

Unlike a traditional backlog, this document is not a collection of random ideas.

Every item included here has been reviewed and aligns with Scout's long-term product vision.

The purpose of this document is to guide future product evolution while ensuring that new capabilities remain consistent with Scout's philosophy and design principles.

This document is the catalog of individually approved enhancements. The broader, phased long-term direction they roll up into is described in [FUTURE_UI_ROADMAP.md](FUTURE_UI_ROADMAP.md); where an enhancement below also appears there, only one of the two documents should carry the full description - see that document's Phase 2 and Phase 4 sections for the current cross-references (EP-011, EP-009).

# Prioritization Framework

Enhancements are categorized using the following priorities.

## High Priority

Features that significantly improve the core user experience or provide immediate business value.

## Medium Priority

Important enhancements that improve workflow, usability, or intelligence, but are not blockers for the core platform.

## Long-Term Vision

Ambitious capabilities that represent the future direction of Scout and may require significant engineering effort.

## Research Ideas

Concepts that require additional validation, user research, or technical investigation before implementation.

# Backlog Management Guidelines

When adding new enhancements:

- Ensure alignment with the Scout Vision.
- Avoid duplicate functionality.
- Prioritize user value over technical novelty.
- Focus on helping users answer:
  - What is happening?
  - Why does it matter?
  - What should I do next?
  - What opportunities should I pursue?

Every enhancement should support Scout's mission of becoming an AI-powered sales strategist rather than simply another AI tool.
