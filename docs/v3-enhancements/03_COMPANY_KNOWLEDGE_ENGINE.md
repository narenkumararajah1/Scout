# Company Knowledge Engine

## Purpose

The Company Knowledge Engine is the foundation of Scout's next evolution.

Its purpose is to give Scout a comprehensive understanding of Innominds by building a centralized knowledge base that powers every AI-generated recommendation, report, meeting brief, sales playbook, and outreach draft.

Rather than relying solely on an LLM's general knowledge, Scout should answer questions and generate recommendations using trusted Innominds knowledge.

---

# Vision

Scout should understand Innominds as well as an experienced Solutions Architect or Senior Sales Executive.

Every recommendation should be grounded in actual company knowledge rather than generic AI reasoning.

The Company Knowledge Engine becomes the primary source of organizational context throughout Scout.

---

# Objectives

The Company Knowledge Engine should:

- Understand Innominds' services.
- Understand industry expertise.
- Understand technical capabilities.
- Understand solution offerings.
- Understand case studies.
- Understand customer success stories.
- Understand whitepapers.
- Understand brochures.
- Understand accelerators.
- Understand delivery models.
- Understand technology partnerships.
- Understand domain expertise.

Every future AI capability should consume this knowledge.

---

# Knowledge Sources

The knowledge engine should support multiple source types.

Examples include:

- Official Innominds website
- Service pages
- Practice pages
- Industry pages
- PDF brochures
- Solution briefs
- Whitepapers
- Case studies
- Technical documentation
- Capability presentations
- Marketing collateral
- Internal documentation (where permitted)

Additional knowledge sources should be easily added over time.

---

# Knowledge Architecture

The knowledge pipeline should follow a consistent flow.

```
Knowledge Source
        ↓
Document Processing
        ↓
Content Extraction
        ↓
Chunking
        ↓
Embeddings
        ↓
Vector Storage
        ↓
Semantic Retrieval
        ↓
LLM Context
        ↓
Scout Response
```

The retrieval pipeline should remain independent of any specific LLM.

---

# Core Capabilities

The engine should support:

- Website ingestion
- PDF ingestion
- Batch document ingestion
- Incremental updates
- Duplicate detection
- Version tracking
- Metadata extraction
- Semantic search
- Hybrid retrieval
- Source attribution

The system should maintain a searchable and explainable knowledge repository.

---

# Knowledge Categories

Knowledge should be organized into logical categories.

Examples:

- Services
- Industries
- Solutions
- Technologies
- Accelerators
- Platforms
- Partnerships
- Case Studies
- Customer Success
- Thought Leadership
- Company Information

Categories should simplify retrieval and improve recommendation quality.

---

# Metadata

Every document should include metadata.

Examples:

- Title
- Category
- Source
- Author (if available)
- Publish date
- Version
- Tags
- Industry
- Technology
- Last updated

Metadata should improve search accuracy and filtering.

---

# Knowledge Retrieval

Scout should retrieve only the most relevant knowledge for a task.

Examples:

Generating a Healthcare report should prioritize:

- Healthcare case studies
- Healthcare solutions
- Healthcare partnerships
- Relevant accelerators

Rather than retrieving unrelated company information.

---

# AI Integration

Every AI-generated artifact should automatically use the Company Knowledge Engine.

This includes:

- Intelligence Reports
- Meeting Briefs
- Sales Playbooks
- Outreach Drafts
- Ask Scout
- Scout Copilot
- Executive Intelligence

Knowledge retrieval should become a standard part of every AI workflow.

---

# Explainability

Every recommendation should reference supporting company knowledge where appropriate.

Examples:

- Relevant service offering
- Similar customer engagement
- Applicable case study
- Supporting whitepaper
- Technology expertise

Users should understand why Scout recommends a particular solution.

---

# Knowledge Quality

The system should prioritize:

- Accuracy
- Freshness
- Relevance
- Traceability
- Explainability

Outdated knowledge should be refreshable without rebuilding the entire knowledge base.

---

# Administration

Administrators should be able to:

- Add knowledge sources
- Remove outdated content
- Refresh knowledge
- View ingestion status
- View processing history
- Monitor indexing progress

Knowledge management should remain straightforward as the repository grows.

---

# Success Criteria

The Company Knowledge Engine succeeds when every AI-generated recommendation feels specific to Innominds rather than generic.

Users should consistently see relevant services, industry expertise, case studies, and supporting evidence integrated into Scout's recommendations.
