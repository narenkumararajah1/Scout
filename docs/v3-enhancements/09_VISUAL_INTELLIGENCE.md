# Visual Intelligence

## Purpose

Visual Intelligence transforms Scout from a text-heavy sales intelligence platform into an executive-grade decision support system.

Rather than presenting large amounts of written information, Scout should communicate business insights through meaningful visualizations, trends, dashboards, and timelines.

Every visualization should help users understand what is happening before they begin reading detailed analysis.

---

# Vision

Visual Intelligence should become the primary method through which users understand companies.

Rather than reading lengthy reports, users should immediately recognize:

- Company direction
- Business momentum
- Technology evolution
- Hiring patterns
- Executive movement
- Opportunity growth
- Strategic priorities

The objective is to reduce cognitive load while improving decision making.

---

# Design Philosophy

Every page should answer a question visually before answering it with text.

Visualizations should support—not replace—the written intelligence.

Every chart should help users answer:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- Why does it matter?
- What should I do next?

---

# Objectives

Visual Intelligence should:

- Improve readability
- Reduce information overload
- Surface trends immediately
- Highlight important business events
- Explain opportunity evolution
- Improve executive presentations
- Support better sales decisions

Every visualization should communicate business value rather than decorative statistics.

---

- Industry
- Global presence
- Business growth

- Cloud maturity

- Opportunity score

- Employee growth

- Hiring trends
- Executive changes
- Business expansion
- Geographic growth
- Investment activity

Trend visualizations should clearly distinguish long-term patterns from isolated events.

# Executive Intelligence

- Partnerships
- Hiring spikes

Examples:

- Engineering centers

# Comparative Intelligence

- Opportunity volume

# Interactive Exploration

Potential capabilities include:

- Drill-down
- Expand details
- Filter timeline
- Compare time periods
- Highlight related intelligence

---

Visual Intelligence should become a standard component of:

- Intelligence Reports
- Company Intelligence
- Executive Intelligence
- Dashboard
- Opportunity Analysis
- Meeting Briefs
- Scout Copilot

---

# Design Principles

Every visualization should be:

- Meaningful
- Explainable
- Interactive
- Business-focused
- Executive-friendly
- Consistent
- Accessible

Visualizations should simplify complex information rather than add unnecessary complexity.

---

# Success Criteria

Visual Intelligence succeeds when users can understand a company's business direction within a few seconds of opening its profile.

Charts should communicate insights rather than simply display data.

Every visualization should help users make faster and better sales decisions.
