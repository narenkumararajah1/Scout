# LinkedIn Intelligence

## Purpose

LinkedIn Intelligence extends Scout's understanding of customer organizations by incorporating professional network insights, executive activity, organizational growth, hiring trends, and relationship opportunities.

Rather than viewing LinkedIn as another social media platform, Scout should treat it as a strategic source of business intelligence that supports account planning and relationship building.

The goal is to help sales teams identify the right people, the right timing, and the strongest path into every customer organization.

---

# Vision

Scout should leverage LinkedIn intelligence to strengthen customer relationships, identify decision makers, monitor organizational change, and uncover new sales opportunities.

The platform should help users understand not only who a company is, but who the people behind that company are.

---

# Objectives

LinkedIn Intelligence should help Scout understand:

- Executive leadership
- Organizational structure
- Hiring activity
- Employee growth
- Job changes
- Promotions
- Professional connections
- Company updates
- Thought leadership
- Industry engagement

This intelligence should enrich company profiles without replacing existing Company Intelligence.

---

# Relationship Intelligence

Scout should identify relationship opportunities.

Potential insights include:

- Mutual connections
- Shared organizations
- Shared industries
- Shared universities
- Previous employers
- Existing customer relationships
- Internal Innominds contacts

Relationship intelligence should help users determine the strongest path into an organization.

# Company Activity

# Ethical and Technical Considerations

LinkedIn Intelligence must respect platform policies, user privacy, and applicable licensing agreements.

The implementation should rely on approved integrations and authorized data sources.

If certain information cannot be obtained through supported APIs, Scout should clearly indicate those limitations rather than attempting unsupported collection methods.

---

- Company Intelligence
- Executive Intelligence
- Meeting Briefs

- AI Outreach
- Reports
